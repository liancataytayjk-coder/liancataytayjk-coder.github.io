Lead project · Public health analytics · Forecasting · Risk scoring

EpiWatch Mindanao Public Health Risk Forecasting

A simulated public health early warning system with case forecasting, anomaly detection, risk scoring, and dashboard-ready outputs.

Context. Public health teams need a structured way to monitor disease trends, detect abnormal increases, and prioritize municipalities for validation and preparedness review.

Method. I generated synthetic surveillance data, engineered incidence and lag features, forecasted next-four-week case burden, detected anomalies against local baselines, and built an Early Warning Risk Index.

Findings. The prototype produced 3,024 municipality-week records and classified the latest week into 1 High Risk, 1 Alert, 14 Watch, and 8 Normal municipalities. Jolo ranked highest with a 0.80 risk index after a sharp rise above its baseline, and the forecasting model’s MAE was 36.76 cases.

Tools: Python, Pandas, NumPy, Excel, Power BI-ready CSV
Code: github.com/liancataytayjk-coder/epiwatch-mindanao

Economics & Research · Agricultural economics · Synthetic control method · Impact evaluation

CARP and Agricultural Productivity: A Synthetic Control Study

A synthetic control evaluation of how the 1988 agrarian reform program affected Philippine labor productivity, land productivity, and TFP.

Context. The Comprehensive Agrarian Reform Program (CARP), launched in 1988, is central to Philippine debates on equity and rural development, but its effect on national agricultural productivity is rarely tested against a credible counterfactual.

Method. I used the synthetic control method (SCM) to build a “synthetic Philippines” that matches pre-1988 productivity trends, then compared post-reform labor productivity, land productivity, and TFP. A GDP per capita placebo checked whether the gaps were specific to agriculture.

Findings. From 1989 to 2008, Philippine agriculture ran below its synthetic control: labor productivity was about 18.2% lower, land productivity 16.1% lower, and TFP about 9% lower. GDP per capita showed only a 2.5% gap, so the shortfall is specific to agriculture and driven mainly by slower TFP growth.

Tools: R, R Markdown, Synthetic control, TFP analysis
Code: github.com/liancataytayjk-coder/agricultural-scm-productivity-analysis

Economics & Research · Macroeconomics · Migration · ARDL modeling

Philippine Emigration ARDL Macroeconomic Analysis

An econometric analysis of how macroeconomic conditions relate to Philippine emigration trends.

Context. I explored how macroeconomic conditions relate to Philippine emigration trends.

Method. I used Stata, ARDL bounds testing, ECM modeling, and time-series diagnostics.

Findings. Five of seven ARDL specifications showed a long-run relationship. In the baseline model, a 1% rise in Philippine GDP per capita is linked to a 0.46% fall in long-run emigration, and error-correction terms of −0.56 to −1.00 mean most deviations close within a year. COVID-19 produced a large negative level shift.

Tools: Stata, ARDL bounds testing, ECM modeling, Time-series diagnostics
Code: github.com/liancataytayjk-coder/philippine-emigration-ardl-macroeconomic-analysis

Media Insight Report: Philippine Inflation and Policy Debate

A media insight report turning coverage of the 2026 inflation spike into risks and opportunities for a retail client.

Context. Philippine headline inflation rose from 2.0% in January to a three-year-high 7.2% in April 2026. I wanted to show how media coverage of that spike translates into concrete risks and opportunities for a business client.

Method. I coded coverage into themes (cost-of-living hardship, wage justice versus business costs, and social protection as a shock absorber), read tone qualitatively, and mapped stakeholders for a retail and FMCG client persona.

Findings. Coverage centered on two policy levers: wage hikes of up to P200 a day and expanded social protection, including P43.18B released for cash aid and related programs. For the client, that meant short-term cost and wage pressure, reputational risk around pricing, and medium-term opportunities to align with social protection priorities.

Tools: Excel, Media monitoring, Thematic coding, Data visualization
Code: github.com/liancataytayjk-coder/media-audience-engagement-analysis
