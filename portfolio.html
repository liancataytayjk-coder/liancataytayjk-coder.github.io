Lead project · Agricultural economics · Synthetic control method · Impact evaluation

CARP and Agricultural Productivity: A Synthetic Control Study

Did the 1988 agrarian reform change farm productivity? A synthetic control comparison for labor, land and TFP.

Context. The Comprehensive Agrarian Reform Program (CARP) began in 1988 and still shapes Philippine debates on equity and rural development. Its effect on national farm productivity has rarely been tested against a credible counterfactual.

Method. I used the synthetic control method to build a “synthetic Philippines” that tracks the real one before 1988, then compared the two on labor productivity, land productivity and TFP after the reform. A placebo run on GDP per capita checked whether the gaps were specific to agriculture.

Findings. From 1989 to 2008 Philippine agriculture ran below its synthetic control: labor productivity by about 18.2%, land productivity by 16.1% and TFP by about 9%. GDP per capita was only 2.5% below, so the shortfall belongs to agriculture, and most of it comes from slower TFP growth.

Tools: R, R Markdown, Synthetic control, TFP analysis
Code: github.com/liancataytayjk-coder/agricultural-scm-productivity-analysis

E-commerce Customer Retention Analytics

Cohorts and customer segments from the Olist marketplace, built to show where retention effort pays off.

eCommerce Marketplace Ads Performance Dashboard

Ads data from Amazon, Google, TikTok and eBay in one model, with ACoS/TACoS, stock checks and budget calls.

Context. Marketplace ad teams often juggle separate exports from Amazon Ads, Google Ads, TikTok Ads and eBay Ads, which makes it hard to compare spend, revenue, profit and stock across them.

Method. I merged the four platforms into one schema, cleaned SKU and campaign fields, joined product and inventory data, calculated ACoS/TACoS-style KPIs and added checks that catch reporting errors.

Findings. The model covers 42,360 ad rows and 6,500 sales rows, with $86.7M in attributed ad revenue and an average ROAS of 6.80. It flagged 4,903 campaign and SKU rows to cut or pause.

Tools: Excel, SQL, Python, Streamlit
Code: github.com/liancataytayjk-coder/ecommerce-marketplace-ads-performance-dashboard

CRM & Event Marketing Data Hygiene System

Cleans a messy CRM export: duplicates merged, contacts checked, and event follow-ups ready to send.

Context. Marketing and sales teams often work from CRM exports full of duplicate contacts, bad emails, inconsistent phone numbers and half-filled event records.

Method. I standardized contact fields, scored how complete each record was, merged duplicate groups, matched event registrations to contacts and prepared the follow-up lists.

AI Customer Churn Intelligence System

A churn model that explains itself: risk scores, SHAP drivers and a Power BI view for the retention team.

Context. I wanted a churn model that is accurate and can also say why it flagged each customer.

Method. Classification models in scikit-learn, SHAP to explain each prediction, and Power BI for the reporting.

Findings. The model scored 1,409 customers. The ones it placed in the High Risk group churned at 78.8%; the Low Risk group churned at 12.9%. Contract type, pricing and service usage drove most of the risk, and $27,972 in revenue was flagged as at risk.

Tools: Python, scikit-learn, SHAP, Power BI
Code: github.com/liancataytayjk-coder/ai-customer-churn-intelligence-system

Machine Learning & Risk · Banking analytics · Credit risk · Financial performance

Banking Risk and Financial Performance Analysis

Which borrowers default? A classification model and five risk bands on a 2,000-loan portfolio.

Context. I wanted to find which borrower and loan traits go with default.

Method. A classification model in Python and Pandas, with feature importance. I then split the loans into five equal risk bands and estimated the expected loss in each.

Findings. The model reached a ROC AUC of 0.72. Loans in the Very High band defaulted at 53% and those in the Very Low band at 2%, and the Very High band held 53% of expected-loss exposure. Credit score and interest rate mattered most.

Tools: Python, Pandas, Classification analytics, Feature importance
Code: github.com/liancataytayjk-coder/banking-risk-and-financial-performance-analysis

Economics & Research · Macroeconomics · Migration · ARDL modeling

Philippine Emigration ARDL Macroeconomic Analysis

ARDL models of how income, government spending, labor share and the exchange rate relate to Philippine emigration.

Context. I wanted to see how the Philippine economy relates to emigration over time.

Method. ARDL bounds tests, error-correction models and time-series diagnostics in Stata.

Findings. Five of seven ARDL specifications found a long-run relationship. In the baseline model, a 1% rise in Philippine GDP per capita goes with a 0.46% fall in emigration in the long run. Error-correction terms of −0.56 to −1.00 mean most shocks fade within a year. COVID-19 shows up as a large downward shift.

Tools: Stata, ARDL bounds testing, ECM modeling, Time-series diagnostics
Code: github.com/liancataytayjk-coder/philippine-emigration-ardl-macroeconomic-analysis

Economics & Research · Media insights · Thematic analysis · Policy risk

Media Insight Report: Philippine Inflation and Policy Debate

How news coverage of the 2026 inflation spike turns into risks and openings for a retail client.

Context. Philippine headline inflation rose from 2.0% in January to 7.2% in April 2026, its highest in three years. I wanted to show how news coverage of that rise turns into specific risks and openings for a business.

Method. I coded the coverage into three themes (cost-of-living hardship, wage justice against business costs, and social protection as a shock absorber), read its tone, and mapped stakeholders for a retail and FMCG client.

Findings. Coverage focused on two policy levers: wage increases of up to P200 a day, and more social protection, including P43.18B released for cash aid and related programs. For the client that meant cost and wage pressure in the short term, a reputational risk around pricing, and, further out, room to line up with social protection priorities.

Tools: Excel, Media monitoring, Thematic coding, Data visualization
Code: github.com/liancataytayjk-coder/media-audience-engagement-analysis
