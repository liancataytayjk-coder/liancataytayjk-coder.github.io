Lian Cataytay

Data Analyst · Economist

About

Lian is a data analyst from the Philippines, with a degree in Economics from the University of Southeastern Philippines.

Data is only valuable when it leads to better decisions. Many organizations already have the numbers; what they lack is a clear way to interpret them. That is the gap he focuses on closing.

His work spans econometrics, business intelligence, and machine learning. He has built projects involving retention analytics, AI-powered churn prediction, banking risk analysis, and workforce intelligence.

Lian works with tools such as Python, Power BI, Tableau, Stata, R, and lots of Excel to turn raw datasets into dashboards and models, and further down the line, outputs that are practical, explainable, and decision-ready.

He is especially interested in impact evaluation and using AI-assisted analytics workflows.

Outside work, Lian jogs before the sun is fully up and sings like no one is listening. Sometimes both on the same morning.
