Projects

Twelve projects in business intelligence, machine learning and economic research. Eleven link to their code.

  1. NLP · Power BI Customer Sentiment Intelligence Dashboard Turns 500 customer reviews in more than one language into sentiment, themes and an NPS a manager can act on. An NPS of 30 across 500 multilingual reviews.
  2. Customer analytics · Tableau E-commerce Customer Retention Analytics Cohorts and customer segments from the Olist marketplace, built to show where retention effort pays off. 59.1% churn across 93,358 customers.
  3. Marketing analytics eCommerce Marketplace Ads Performance Dashboard Ads data from Amazon, Google, TikTok and eBay in one model, with ACoS/TACoS, stock checks and budget calls. A 6.80 average ROAS on $86.7M in ad revenue.
  4. FP&A · Forecasting FP&A Revenue, Cost & Expense Forecasting Dashboard Monthly budget-versus-actual reporting, forecasts, scenarios and an anomaly list for month-end review. 98.2% forecast accuracy on $281.6M in revenue.
  5. Machine learning · SHAP AI Customer Churn Intelligence System A churn model that explains itself: risk scores, SHAP drivers and a Power BI view for the retention team. 78.8% churn in the High Risk segment, against 12.9% in Low Risk.
  6. CRM · Data quality CRM & Event Marketing Data Hygiene System Cleans a messy CRM export: duplicates merged, contacts checked, and event follow-ups ready to send. 313 duplicates removed, leaving 1,187 clean contacts.
  7. Impact evaluation · R CARP and Agricultural Productivity: A Synthetic Control Study Did the 1988 agrarian reform change farm productivity? A synthetic control comparison for labor, land and TFP. Labor productivity about 18.2% below its synthetic control.
  8. Labor economics Philippines Labor Market Trend Analysis Employment, underemployment and the shift between sectors in Philippine labor force data. Underemployment at 11.4%, almost three times unemployment.
  9. Media insights Media Insight Report: Philippine Inflation and Policy Debate How news coverage of the 2026 inflation spike turns into risks and openings for a retail client. 15 sources on inflation’s climb to a three-year-high 7.2%.
  10. Time-series econometrics · Stata Philippine Emigration ARDL Macroeconomic Analysis ARDL models of how income, government spending, labor share and the exchange rate relate to Philippine emigration. A 1% rise in GDP per capita, a 0.46% fall in long-run emigration.
  11. Credit risk · Classification Banking Risk and Financial Performance Analysis Which borrowers default? A classification model and five risk bands on a 2,000-loan portfolio. 0.72 ROC AUC; 53% vs 2% default by risk band.
  12. HR analytics Employee Attrition Risk and Workforce Intelligence Analysis Which teams are most likely to lose people, scored from HR and performance data. 21.6% estimated attrition risk across employee groups.
