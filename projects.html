Projects

Thirteen end-to-end projects across business intelligence, machine learning, and economic research. Each one links to its code.

  1. Public health · Forecasting EpiWatch Mindanao Public Health Risk Forecasting A simulated public health early warning system with case forecasting, anomaly detection, risk scoring, and dashboard-ready outputs. 1 High Risk, 1 Alert, and 14 Watch municipalities in the latest week.
  2. NLP · Power BI Customer Sentiment Intelligence Dashboard Sentiment analysis of customer reviews and business-facing metrics for interpreting customer pain points and satisfaction trends. An NPS of 30 across 500 multilingual reviews.
  3. Customer analytics · Tableau E-commerce Customer Retention Analytics Customer behavior analysis focused on retention, segmentation, cohort patterns, and churn-risk insights. 59.1% churn across 93,358 customers.
  4. Marketing analytics eCommerce Marketplace Ads Performance Dashboard Marketplace ads reporting with ACoS/TACoS KPIs, slow-moving SKU analysis, data quality checks, and budget recommendations. A 6.80 average ROAS on $86.7M in ad revenue.
  5. FP&A · Forecasting FP&A Revenue, Cost & Expense Forecasting Dashboard Monthly FP&A reporting with budget variance, forecast accuracy, KPI trends, scenario analysis, and anomaly review. 98.2% forecast accuracy on $281.6M in revenue.
  6. Machine learning · SHAP AI Customer Churn Intelligence System An explainable churn-prediction workflow using machine learning, SHAP, and retention-focused reporting. 78.8% churn in the High Risk segment, against 12.9% in Low Risk.
  7. CRM · Data quality CRM & Event Marketing Data Hygiene System CRM cleaning, duplicate resolution, contact validation, event follow-up, and marketing-ready outputs. 313 duplicates removed, leaving 1,187 clean contacts.
  8. Impact evaluation · R CARP and Agricultural Productivity: A Synthetic Control Study A synthetic control evaluation of how the 1988 agrarian reform program affected Philippine labor productivity, land productivity, and TFP. Labor productivity about 18.2% below its synthetic control.
  9. Labor economics Philippines Labor Market Trend Analysis A labor-market analysis of employment quality, workforce composition, and underemployment patterns in the Philippines. Underemployment at 11.4%, almost three times unemployment.
  10. Media insights Media Insight Report: Philippine Inflation and Policy Debate A media insight report turning coverage of the 2026 inflation spike into risks and opportunities for a retail client. 15 sources on inflation’s climb to a three-year-high 7.2%.
  11. Time-series econometrics · Stata Philippine Emigration ARDL Macroeconomic Analysis An econometric analysis of how macroeconomic conditions relate to Philippine emigration trends. A 1% rise in GDP per capita, a 0.46% fall in long-run emigration.
  12. Credit risk · Classification Banking Risk and Financial Performance Analysis A financial-risk project identifying borrower and loan characteristics associated with default exposure. 0.72 ROC AUC; 53% vs 2% default by risk band.
  13. HR analytics Employee Attrition Risk and Workforce Intelligence Analysis A workforce intelligence project identifying employee groups and teams with stronger attrition-risk patterns. 21.6% estimated attrition risk across employee groups.
