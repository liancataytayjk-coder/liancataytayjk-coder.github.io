CRM & Event Marketing Data Hygiene System

  • Excel
  • Power Query logic
  • Python

Cleaned master database 1,187 unique contacts retained from 1,500 messy CRM records.
Duplicate resolution 313 records removed using email, mobile, and name-city matching.
Follow-up readiness 310 referrals prepared for sales and marketing follow-up.

Context

Marketing and sales teams often work from CRM exports with duplicate contacts, invalid emails, inconsistent phone numbers, and incomplete event records.

Data

Synthetic CRM, event registration, referral, and testimonial datasets.

Method

I standardized contact fields, scored record completeness, resolved duplicate groups, matched event registrations, and prepared post-event follow-up outputs.

Output

A cleaned master contact database, duplicate audit table, event tracker, referral tracker, testimonial extracts, and Excel workbook summaries.

Findings

The workflow cleaned 1,500 raw CRM records into 1,187 unique contacts, removed 313 duplicates, flagged 469 invalid emails, and prepared 310 referrals for follow-up.
