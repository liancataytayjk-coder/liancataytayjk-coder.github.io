FP&A Revenue, Cost & Expense Forecasting Dashboard

  • Excel
  • SQL
  • Python

Monthly FP&A model $281.6M total synthetic revenue across 36 reporting months.
Forecast accuracy 98.2% average forecast accuracy across actual vs forecast outputs.
Anomaly review 369 financial movements flagged for month-end management review.

Context

FP&A teams need reliable monthly reporting to understand revenue trends, cost movement, expense control, forecast accuracy, and KPI performance.

Data

Synthetic monthly actuals, budget plans, forecast plans, KPI targets, business unit mappings, and expense category mappings.

Method

I standardized messy finance data, calculated actual vs budget and actual vs forecast variances, built scenario forecasts, tracked KPI movement, and flagged anomalies.

Output

An Excel FP&A model, dashboard-ready financials, SQL reporting queries, Streamlit dashboard, Power BI build guide, DAX measures, executive summary, and portfolio case study.

Findings

The workflow analyzed $281.6M in revenue, 50.8% gross margin, 98.2% forecast accuracy, 71 unfavorable variances, and 369 anomaly records for review.
