Philippine Emigration ARDL Macroeconomic Analysis

ARDL models of how income, government spending, labor share and the exchange rate relate to Philippine emigration.

By Lian Cataytay · Stata, ARDL bounds testing, ECM modeling, Time-series diagnostics

Context

I wanted to see how the Philippine economy relates to emigration over time.

Data

Annual Philippine emigration, GDP per capita, government consumption, labor share and PHP–USD exchange rate series from 1981 to 2023, with matching US series.

Method

ARDL bounds tests, error-correction models and time-series diagnostics in Stata.

Output

The fitted models, ARDL output tables and notes on the migration data.

Findings

Five of seven ARDL specifications found a long-run relationship. In the baseline model, a 1% rise in Philippine GDP per capita goes with a 0.46% fall in emigration in the long run. Error-correction terms of −0.56 to −1.00 mean most shocks fade within a year. COVID-19 shows up as a large downward shift.

Reflection

Next I’d test for structural breaks and compare with other countries.
