Employee Attrition Risk and Workforce Intelligence Analysis

Which teams are most likely to lose people, scored from HR and performance data.

By Lian Cataytay · Python, Pandas, Risk scoring, Hotspot analysis

Context

I wanted to see whether workforce data could point to the employees and teams most likely to leave.

Data

HR and employee performance data.

Method

Attrition risk scoring and hotspot analysis in Python and Pandas.

Output

Risk-scored employee data and team-level notes for managers.

Findings

Estimated attrition risk was 21.6% across the employee groups analyzed, and 9 teams stood out enough to flag for manager review.

Reflection

I’d add a predictive retention model and employee sentiment data.
