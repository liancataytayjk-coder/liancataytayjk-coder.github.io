Employee Attrition Risk and Workforce Intelligence Analysis

A workforce intelligence project identifying employee groups and teams with stronger attrition-risk patterns.

By Lian Cataytay · Python, Pandas, Risk scoring, Hotspot analysis

Context

I explored how workforce data could be used to identify employees and teams with higher attrition risk.

Data

HR and employee-performance datasets.

Method

I used Python, Pandas, attrition-risk scoring, and hotspot analysis.

Output

Attrition-risk datasets and manager-level workforce insights.

Findings

The project identified teams and employee groups showing stronger retention risk patterns.

Reflection

I’d improve this by adding predictive retention modeling and employee-sentiment analysis.
