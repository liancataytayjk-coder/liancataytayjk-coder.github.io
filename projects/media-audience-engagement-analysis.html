Media Insight Report: Philippine Inflation and Policy Debate

A media insight report turning coverage of the 2026 inflation spike into risks and opportunities for a retail client.

By Lian Cataytay · Excel, Media monitoring, Thematic coding, Data visualization

Context

Philippine headline inflation rose from 2.0% in January to a three-year-high 7.2% in April 2026. I wanted to show how media coverage of that spike translates into concrete risks and opportunities for a business client.

Data

15 public media and official sources published between February and May 2026, including national dailies, business media, PSA, DOF, DBM, and BSP releases, and international outlets.

Method

I coded coverage into themes (cost-of-living hardship, wage justice versus business costs, and social protection as a shock absorber), read tone qualitatively, and mapped stakeholders for a retail and FMCG client persona.

Output

A consultant-style media insight report with an inflation timeline, stakeholder map, Excel visualizations, and recommended actions.

Findings

Coverage centered on two policy levers: wage hikes of up to P200 a day and expanded social protection, including P43.18B released for cash aid and related programs. For the client, that meant short-term cost and wage pressure, reputational risk around pricing, and medium-term opportunities to align with social protection priorities.

Reflection

I’d improve this by integrating social-media analytics and interactive dashboards.
