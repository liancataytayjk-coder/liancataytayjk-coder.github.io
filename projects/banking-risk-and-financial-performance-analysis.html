Banking Risk and Financial Performance Analysis

  • Python
  • Pandas
  • Classification analytics
  • Feature importance
Model performance 0.72 ROC AUC for classifying borrower default exposure.
Risk separation 53% vs 2% default rate in the Very High risk band versus the Very Low band.
Expected-loss concentration 53% of expected-loss exposure sits in the top risk band (20% of loans).

Context

I wanted to understand which borrower and loan characteristics are most associated with default risk.

Data

A synthetic 2,000-loan portfolio with borrower, loan, credit-history, and repayment attributes and a 20.8% observed default rate.

Method

I used Python, Pandas, a classification model, and feature-importance analysis, then grouped loans into five equal risk bands and estimated expected-loss exposure.

Output

Risk-scored loan datasets and portfolio-risk summaries.

Findings

The model reached 0.72 ROC AUC. Loans in the Very High risk band defaulted at 53%, against 2% in the Very Low band, and that band carried 53% of expected-loss exposure. Credit score and interest rate were the strongest drivers.
